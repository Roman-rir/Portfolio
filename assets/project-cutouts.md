## Prompt specification

Use case: background-extraction. Edit the supplied hardware photo into an actual alpha-transparent PNG. Remove only the surrounding tabletop or white canvas and visible background through gaps. Preserve the photographed device, wiring, components, colors, camera angle, aspect ratio, framing, and all existing labels and yellow arrows. Keep white text-label boxes opaque for readability. No tint, fade, new background, shadow, border, crop, restyling, or invented hardware details. For the low-resolution robot front view, retain the photographed parts without inventing finer details.

The BPT front cutout also retains the hand and fingers. The annotated interior views retain their text boxes. The two robot views remove the granite tabletop.
