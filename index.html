03 / BIOMEDICAL SYSTEMS

Blood pressure trending (BPT).

A calibrated cuffless monitoring prototype built around an ESP8266, a MAX32664 sensor hub, and optical pulse sensing. Its integrated display shows blood pressure trends, heart rate, SpO2, and temperature.

The rechargeable device combines a 1.8-inch display with a MAX30205 temperature sensor. The project covers sensor integration, firmware, hardware assembly, and comparison with reference measurements.

04 / BIOMEDICAL SIGNAL PROCESSING

Pulse transit time (PTT).

A separate cuffless blood pressure estimation prototype using ECG and PPG signals from a MAX86150 sensor, with an ESP8266 and Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W handling acquisition and processing.

The signal pipeline filters ECG, detects R-peaks and PPG waveform features, and uses their timing for blood pressure estimation. A 3.5-inch touchscreen displays the signals and estimated readings.

Developed alongside BPT as part of the AIUB Best Capstone Project Award-winning work, published at IEEE BECITHCON 2025 and showcased at Broadband Expo 2026 and a Magna Charta Observatory symposium.

05 / EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Vision that moves with you.

A Raspberry Pi smart grocery trolley prototype that follows a shopper carrying a designated QR marker. OpenCV camera processing guides the motors, while an ultrasonic sensor stops movement when an obstacle is within 20 cm.

Integrated HX711 load-cell measurements with weight-encoded QR codes on a TFT display and a Flask interface for live video, manual control, and scan logging. Weight measurement and QR-guided following were demonstrated; weight-verification scans used a phone after a camera module failed.

06 / MUSIC CONTEXT01 / 02

06 / AUDIO & MACHINE LEARNING

Finding structure in sound.

A CSE715 research project in PyTorch exploring music tagging and audio-text retrieval on MusicCaps. Modeled one-second audio segments with GraphSAGE, encoded captions with DistilBERT, and compared frozen multimodal fusion with text-only and audio-only baselines.

Across 3,964 usable audio clips, frozen fusion did not improve Macro-F1 over the text-only model. Contrastive training raised text-to-graph Recall@10 from 0.83% to 17.77% on 606 held-out pairs; a pretrained CLAP model reached 45.38%.
