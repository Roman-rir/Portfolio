Silicon, signals,

I'm an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from AIUB, currently pursuing a Master of Science in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at BRAC University. My work spans RTL/VLSI design, real-time data acquisition, embedded systems, biomedical signal processing, and AI automation.
